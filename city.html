Edmonton at a Glance

Edmonton is a vibrant city that combines natural scenery with urban attractions, making it a great destination for short trips or weekend travel. The river valley offers extensive trails and viewpoints that feel surprisingly close to downtown. The city also has lively neighbourhoods with local restaurants, cafés, and shops that are easy to explore on foot.

What Visitors Enjoy

  • Walk through the River Valley trail system
  • Visit local museums and galleries
  • Explore Whyte Avenue shops and restaurants
  • Attend festivals and seasonal events

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Edmonton is from late spring to early fall, when the weather is warmer and the city hosts many outdoor festivals. Summer offers long daylight hours, perfect for exploring the river valley and local events. Winter can be very cold, but it also brings unique activities like ice skating and winter festivals, and the view of the city during winter is otherwordly.

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Start the morning with a walk in the River Valley and just enjoy the calming view.
  2. Visit the Royal Alberta Museum before lunch.
  3. Have lunch with a bunch of tasty options everywhere to explore shops.
  4. End the day with dinner and sunset views downtown, the bridge is especially beautiful during the night.
